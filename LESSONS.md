Lessons (hints until the next batch repeats them):
1. **Views come from the Shorts feed (95%+), not search.** Opposite of Chunav Diary. Frame 1 and the first 2 s decide reach; search-phrase titles matter less.
2. **Hold through the middle is what separates winners.** Ramayana kept 85% at the mid-point; Delhi and Alice fell to about 50% by 40%. Fix the flat middle: put the reveal/number earlier and change the picture at second 5-8.
3. **Everyone leaves in the last 10-20%.** All reels drop sharply at 90%. Cut the outro/ask to under 1.5 s and loop straight back to frame 1.
4. **Zero feed push = not a content problem.** Galaxy held 62% but got no feed views at all, same pattern as Pawan/U19/Fable on 11 Nov. Check Studio flags (copyright/limited) before judging the content.
5. **Shares 0, subs 2 from ~4,000 views.** The "send this to the friend who said X" ask isn't landing yet; test a comment question instead, and add a follow reason (series line).
