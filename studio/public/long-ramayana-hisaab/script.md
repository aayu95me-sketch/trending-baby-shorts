## 1. Ab tak kitna kamaya (0:45 to 2:30)

[V: chapter card "1. ab tak ki kamai"; then our own animated bar chart, one bar per day, India net]

Film 5 November ko aayi. Director Nitesh Tiwari, aur Ranbir Kapoor, Yash, Sai Pallavi.

Pehle din India mein 64.85 crore net. Doosre din, Friday ko, girke 48. Weekend pe phir 70 aur 56.

[V: the Day 5 bar shoots up, gold, Diwali lights photo behind it]

Phir aaya 9 November, Diwali ke agle din ka Monday. 107.5 crore. Ek din mein. Ye film ka ab tak ka sabse bada din hai. Agle din bhi 94.

Pehle aath din mein India net 564.6 crore.

[V: second-weekend bars 48, 73, 75 light up]

Asli test doosra weekend hota hai, jab tyohaar ka josh utar jaata hai. Ramayana ne doosre Friday-Saturday-Sunday mein 196 crore aur kamaaye. Matlab log sirf Diwali ki chhutti mein nahi, recommendation sunke bhi aa rahe the.

[V: Monday 32, Tuesday 28.7 bars, colour turns grey]

Phir Monday 32 aur Tuesday 28.7. Yahan pehli baar film dheemi hui. Chhutiyaan khatam, school aur office wapas.

[V: four number cards: India net 821 · India gross 981.5 · Overseas 502 · Worldwide 1,483.5]

Terah din ka total: India net 821 crore, India gross 981.5 crore, videsh se 502 crore. Worldwide, 1,483.5 crore.

Ek cheez yaad rakhna, aage kaam aayegi. Gross matlab ticket ka poora paisa. Net matlab usme se GST nikal ke. Aur jo paisa producer tak pahunchta hai, wo in dono se kaafi kam hota hai. Wo hisaab chapter 5 mein.

## 2. Paisa kahan se aa raha hai (2:30 to 3:45)

[V: chapter card "2. paisa kahan se"; stacked bar of the 821 crore by language]

India ke 821 crore mein se 627 crore sirf Hindi version se aaye. Yaani 76 pratishat.

Kannada 67 crore. Yahan Yash ka asar dikhta hai. Telugu 52, Tamil 38, Malayalam 37.

[V: side by side bars: Ramayana Hindi share 76% vs Pushpa 2 Hindi share 66%]

Ab ek comparison jo kisi ne nahi dikhaya. Pushpa 2 Telugu film thi, phir bhi uske 1,234 crore India net mein se 812 crore Hindi dub se aaye the, lagbhag 66 pratishat. Ramayana Hindi film hai, aur uska Hindi hissa 76 pratishat hai.

Matlab Ramayana ka engine Hindi belt hai. Aur aage badhne ki jagah do hi hain: South, aur videsh.

[V: world map, glowing dots on markets; "34% overseas"]

Videsh se 502 crore, kul kamai ka 34 pratishat. Aur doosre weekend pe film chhe naye deshon mein khuli, jinme Indonesia aur Mexico bhi hain. Ye abhi poori tarah gina bhi nahi gaya hai.

## 3. Aur kitna kamayegi? Humara hisaab (3:45 to 5:45)

[V: chapter card "3. humara hisaab"; two blocks: week 1 vs week 2]

Ab asli kaam. Trade wale bolte hain "2,000 crore jayegi". Kis base pe? Hum base dikhayenge.

Pehle saat din mein India net 511 crore aaya. Agle saat din mein, Wednesday ki advance booking jod ke, lagbhag 337 crore. Yaani doosre hafte ne pehle hafte ka 66 pratishat rakha. Badi filmon ke liye ye mazboot hold hai.

[V: three lines fanning out into the future: grey 40%, gold 50%, green 55%]

Iske baad har hafta pichhle hafte ka kitna rakhega? Humne teen case liye.

Dheema case: har hafta pichhle ka 40 pratishat. Beech ka case: 50 pratishat. Mazboot case: 55 pratishat.

[V: result table builds row by row]

Dheema case mein India net lagbhag 1,070 crore pe rukti hai, aur worldwide lagbhag 1,890 crore.
Beech ke case mein India net lagbhag 1,180 crore, worldwide lagbhag 2,080 crore.
Mazboot case mein India net lagbhag 1,260 crore, worldwide lagbhag 2,200 crore.

Isme China jaisa koi bada naya market nahi gina. Aur videsh ki kamai thodi dheere badhti maani hai.

[V: small stamp "hisaab, bhavishyavani nahi"]

Ye bhavishyavani nahi hai, hisaab hai. Agar teesre hafte ka asli number humare beech wale case se neeche gira, to final number bhi neeche aayega. Hum har hafte pinned comment mein asli number se milayenge.

## 4. Kaunse records tootenge? (5:45 to 7:15)

[V: chapter card "4. records"; all-time worldwide leaderboard, Ramayana's bar climbing]

Ab ye list dekho. Indian filmon ki sabse badi worldwide kamai.

Dangal 2,070 crore. Dhurandhar 2, 1,813. Baahubali 2, 1,788. Pushpa 2, 1,742. Aur Ramayana 13 din mein 1,483 pe, number paanch pe. RRR, KGF 2 aur pehli Dhurandhar ko pehle hi peeche chhod chuki hai.

[V: grey "slow case" marker lands above Dhurandhar 2]

Humare dheeme case mein bhi Ramayana 1,890 tak jaati hai. Yaani Dhurandhar 2, Baahubali 2 aur Pushpa 2, teeno ko paar. Number do pakka dikh raha hai.

[V: gold marker lands right on Dangal's line]

Aur Dangal? Beech wala case 2,080. Dangal 2,070. Das crore ka farak. Ye sach mein sikka uchhaalne jaisa hai.

[V: Dangal's bar splits into two colours: India ~535, overseas ~1,535]

Lekin yahan ek twist hai. Dangal ke 2,070 crore mein se 1,535 crore videsh se aaye the, aur unka bada hissa China se. India ke andar Dangal ki gross kamai lagbhag 535 crore thi.

Ramayana India mein 13 din mein 981 crore gross kar chuki hai. Matlab India ke andar, Ramayana Dangal se pehle hi lagbhag do guna aage hai. Worldwide record ke liye usse ya to China jaisa market chahiye, ya teesre-chauthe hafte mein mazboot rehna padega.

## 5. 4,000 crore ka sach (7:15 to 9:30)

[V: chapter card "5. budget ka sach"; ₹4,000 Cr stack of notes]

Ab doosra sawaal. Producer Namit Malhotra ne ek podcast mein khud kaha tha: Ramayana ke dono parts ka budget 500 million dollar hai, yaani lagbhag 4,000 crore. Business Today ke mutabik isme promotion bhi shaamil hai. Part 1 ka alag kharcha public nahi hai.

[V: one rocket icon x 6.5]

Scale samjho: Chandrayaan-3 mission lagbhag 615 crore ka tha. Ramayana ka budget saadhe chhe Chandrayaan jitna hai.

[V: a ₹100 ticket that splits into pieces, our own animation]

Ab sabse zaroori baat. 1,483 crore producer ki jeb mein nahi jaate. Ek 100 rupaye ka ticket lo. Is film ke data mein gross aur net ka farak dekho to lagbhag 16 rupaye tax mein jaate hain. Bache lagbhag 84 mein se, aam taur pe theatre lagbhag aadha rakhta hai, pehle hafte thoda kam, baad mein zyada. Yaani 100 ke ticket pe film wale ke haath lagbhag 40 se 42 rupaye. Videsh mein bhi film wale ko aam taur pe gross ka lagbhag 40 pratishat milta hai. Ye industry ka rough rule hai, har deal alag hoti hai.

[V: running tally builds: theatre share + OTT = ~₹1,300 Cr]

Ab beech wale case pe ye rule lagao. India net 1,180 ka lagbhag 45 pratishat, lagbhag 530 crore. Videsh ke lagbhag 660 crore ka 40 pratishat, lagbhag 265 crore. Theatre se film wale tak lagbhag 800 crore.

Phir OTT. Report ke mutabik makers ne dono filmon ke liye 700 crore ka offer thukra diya tha, aur lagbhag 1,000 crore chahte hain. Agar 1,000 mila, to Part 1 ke hisse lagbhag 500. Satellite aur music ke number public nahi hain, isliye humne nahi jode.

[V: balance scale: left ~1,300, right ~2,000 "agar budget aadha-aadha bata"]

Total: Part 1 se lagbhag 1,300 crore. Agar 4,000 ka budget dono filmon mein aadha-aadha maano, to Part 1 ka hissa 2,000 hota hai.

To jawab: abhi nahi. Part 1 akela poora paisa wapas nahi laayega. Lekin ye uska kaam tha bhi nahi. 4,000 crore do filmon ka daav hai. Part 1 ne Part 2 ke liye audience bana di hai. Asli hisaab Diwali 2027 mein Part 2 ke baad poora hoga.
