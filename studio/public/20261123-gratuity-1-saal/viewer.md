Who: 22-40 salaried Indians (private jobs, contract/fixed-term staff) who saw "gratuity ab 1 saal mein" forwards; trends ग्रेच्युटी 5K+, কর্মচারী সুবিধা (employee benefits) 5K+, వేతనం (wages) 1K+ on 22 Nov, the day after the labour codes' first anniversary (in force 21 Nov 2025).
Doubt: Does everyone now get gratuity after 1 year instead of 5?
Answer: No. Under the new labour codes the 1-year rule is for fixed-term employees (Goodreturns, Gulf News, Outlook Money, Business Today all agree). Permanent employees still need 5 years of continuous service (Outlook Money, Business Today). Formula: 15/26 x last wages x years (Gulf News, Business Today). Wages = basic + DA (+ retaining allowance per Outlook) must be at least 50% of total pay; excess allowances are added back, so gratuity can rise (Outlook Money, Business Today).
Own calculation: wages Rs 30,000 x 15/26 x 1 year = Rs 17,308 ("lagbhag satrah hazaar").
Not said: the Rs 20 lakh ceiling (no fetched source), the "20-50% higher" estimate (unsourced), the 30-day payment deadline (single source), death/disability exceptions (sources differ).
